Set up before your first invoice

Do these in order. Invoices can only use saved items, so items come before billing.

  1. Company profileLegal name, address and tax number. Your invoices and tax returns print these details.
  2. ItemsEverything you sell, with its price, tax rate and, in India, its HSN or SAC code.
  3. Customers and vendorsTheir tax status and location decide how tax applies to them.
  4. Bank and cashAdd each account with its opening balance, so your cash figures start out right.

Sales

Use a quote while the customer is still deciding, and an invoice once they owe you. Only issued invoices change your books.

FeatureUse it whenDon't use it when
QuoteYou're offering a price the customer can accept or decline.The customer already owes you. Create an invoice.
Invoice draftYou're still preparing an invoice and may change it.The invoice is final. Issue it.
Issue invoiceThe invoice is final. This records the sale and tax, and takes tracked goods out of stock.You don't have enough stock. The app stops you until stock is recorded.
Record paymentA customer paid all or part of an invoice.The invoice is still a draft. Issue it first.
Credit noteYou need to reduce an issued invoice for a return, refund or correction.The whole invoice was a mistake with nothing paid or credited. Void it instead.

Every invoice, quote and credit note line uses a saved item. For a saved service, you can enter start and end dates, which print on the document.

Purchases

Use a bill when a vendor invoices you. Use an expense when you've already paid and there's no bill to track.

FeatureUse it whenDon't use it when
BillA vendor sent an invoice, especially if you're buying stock or paying later.You've paid on the spot for something small. Use an expense.
Record paymentYou paid all or part of a recorded bill.There's no bill yet.
ExpenseYou paid straight away for something like fuel, rent or bank charges.You're buying tracked stock. Expenses don't add to stock, so use a bill.
Paid by the ownerThe owner paid an expense personally. Choose the owner's equity account under Paid from.The company paid. Choose its bank or cash account.

Items and stock

Create one item for each product or service you sell. Its description, price and tax code fill in on each line.

ChoiceUse it when
Goods, stock trackedYou hold physical stock and want quantities and cost of goods sold updated automatically.
Goods, not trackedYou sell a physical product but don't count it.
ServiceYou sell work or time. Its lines can show start and end dates.
HSN or SAC codeYou're in India. Save the code on the item, because invoice lines don't ask for it.
Opening stockYou already hold the goods when you start using JS HUB.

Banking and accounting

Use Banking for money moving through your accounts, and a manual journal only when no other feature fits.

FeatureUse it whenDon't use it when
TransferMoney moved between two of your own accounts, such as bank to petty cash.Money went to a vendor or came from a customer. Record a payment.
ReconcileYou have a bank statement and want to confirm your books match it.You're still changing those entries.
Chart of accountsYou need a new category, like a specific expense type.You just want to label a single transaction. Use its description.
Manual journalYou're recording owner capital, depreciation, accruals or a correction.It's a sale, purchase or stock movement.

Common mix-ups

These four choices cause the most confusion.

Choosing betweenUse the first whenUse the second when

Void or credit noteThe invoice shouldn't exist at allThe invoice was valid but needs reducing or refunding

Your regular routine

  • Daily: issue invoices, record bills and expenses, and enter money in and out.
  • Weekly: check the receivables aging to chase overdue invoices, and the payables aging to plan payments.
  • Monthly: reconcile each bank account against its statement. Reconciled entries lock.
  • Each tax period: file the VAT return in the UAE, or GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B in India.

Fixing mistakes

  • Drafts: edit or delete them freely.
  • Issued invoices and recorded bills: void them. The app posts a reversing entry and keeps the original for your records.
  • If it has payments or credits: delete the payments first. On an invoice, also void its credit notes first.
  • Reducing or refunding a valid sale: issue a credit note instead of voiding.
